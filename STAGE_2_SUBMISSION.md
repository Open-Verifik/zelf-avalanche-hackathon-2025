### What problem are you solving and why does it matter?

**The Password Crisis:**
- Average user has **100+ passwords** but only remembers 5-7
- **81% of data breaches** are due to weak or stolen passwords
- **LastPass breach (2022)**: 30 million users compromised
- **Centralized password managers** = single points of failure

**The Biometric Privacy Problem:**
- Current biometric systems **store face mappings** in databases
- **Apple FaceID, Windows Hello** = centralized biometric storage
- Users lose control of their most personal data
- **No true zero-knowledge biometric solutions exist**

**Why This Matters NOW:**
- **$15 billion password manager market** growing 15% annually
- **Zero-trust security** becoming mandatory for enterprises
- **Web3 adoption** requires better UX for non-crypto users
- **Privacy regulations** (GDPR, CCPA) demanding data ownership

**🎯 Our Revolutionary Solution:**
